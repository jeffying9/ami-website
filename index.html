Positioning

A quality assurance partner, not a sourcing shortcut.

AMI supports companies that need a disciplined path from product intent to reliable manufacturing. Development resources, prototyping methods, and supply chain execution are organized around one priority: making product quality visible, manageable, and repeatable.

The work is suited for engineered products, medical and health-related devices, hardware startups, and teams that need dependable China-based execution without losing control of standards, cost, or project responsibility.

Operating Logic

Quality gates before decisions become expensive.

Define

Clarify product requirements, risk points, supplier constraints, and acceptance standards.

Validate

Use prototypes, pilot builds, and supplier checks to expose technical and process uncertainty.

Control

Translate requirements into inspection criteria, production checkpoints, and accountable follow-up.

Improve

Close quality issues with structured feedback, corrective action, and cost-aware optimization.
